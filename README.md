### Beta HSU (Yun Chu)

Interaction design consultant and creative technologist, Hong Kong/Taipei.

**Working on** bounded agent autonomy, human-in-the-loop pipelines, evaluation as a design method, and accessibility as machine-readability.

Most of my work circles the same question: how human and machine systems distribute transparency, invite participation, and decide who gets to change how they work. I design human-AI systems at scale meant to serve real users, and build smaller ones at night to experiment with their boundaries.

**Currently** AI Agentic Product Owner at [Coseeing](https://coseeing.org), an accessibility organisation, owning the semantic schema and building the entire human-AI pipeline behind [Access8graph](https://github.com/coseeing/accessibility-toolkit). 
**Previously** Design consultant for major FinTech clients at AJA Creative (Taiwan's leading UX agency) and an experience design researcher on AI agent experience at The Hong Kong PolyU.

#### Things I have built

**Access8Graph** — keyboard and speech navigation that lets blind users explore graph data, starting with the Taipei Main Station floor plan.
*Python · at [Coseeing](https://github.com/coseeing) · organisation repo, private, in progress*

Product owner and sole operator of the drawing pipeline, where I set the autonomy line: natural language in, one script that is the only thing allowed to write the XML, automated validation on every edit, and a person at the end for schema-level decisions. Ran multi-model boundary testing to find the gaps in our own spec.

Demoed at [AI Tinkerers Hong Kong - AI for Inclusion](https://hong-kong.aitinkerers.org/p/ai-tinkerers-hong-kong-gba-at-dialogue-in-the-dark-june), June 2026.
Coseeing's shared foundation for these tools is open source at [coseeing/accessibility-toolkit](https://github.com/coseeing/accessibility-toolkit).

<br>

**[llm-guided-quadtree](https://github.com/BetaHsu/llm-guided-quadtree)** — a generative canvas that subdivides and collapses under fixed rules, where a viewer can intervene at any time and a language model can be asked to read the structure, re-tune how it fragments, and explain its intent.
*JavaScript, p5.js, Claude API · [live](https://visionary-machines.vercel.app)*

<br>

**Re-collect** — a social writing platform where another person's work is only partially visible until you rewrite it, or else fades away, and rewriting is what makes it more visible to everyone.
*Angular, TypeScript, Flask, MongoDB · private*

Full-stack, built solo as my final year project at City University of Hong Kong, 2023.
